About the School

The School of Engineering and Technology at Shivaji University, Kolhapur, offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs across core and emerging engineering disciplines.

Located in the Vidyanagar campus, the school benefits from experienced faculty, well‑equipped laboratories, central library access, and active research culture.

Admissions

For UG/PG and Ph.D. programs, check eligibility and submit your enquiry.

Eligibility (Typical)

  • B.Tech: 10+2 with PCM and valid entrance score (e.g., MHT‑CET / JEE).
  • M.Tech: B.Tech/B.E. in relevant branch with valid entrance score.
  • Ph.D.: M.Tech/M.E. or equivalent with valid fellowship/entrance as per university norms.

How to Apply

  • Visit the official portal: sukapps.unishivaji.ac.in
  • Check admission notifications for Engineering & Technology.
  • Fill online application, upload documents, and pay fees.
